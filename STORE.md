## Full description (the big text box)

> _[GIF 1 here: the club reacting to a track — bass pumping, crowd dancing]_

**Pixel DJ is a little pixel-art club that lives on your desk.**

Play internet radio, your own files, or the built-in soundtrack — and the whole scene *moves to it*. Real audio, real reactions: the bass drives the crowd, the highs shimmer the lights, the DJ scratches on the beat. It's not a looping animation faking it; it's an actual FFT reading whatever's playing.

It's also a gentle focus companion. Finish a stretch of work and the DJ nudges you to take a break (breaks are rewarded, not punished). Stay in the zone and the crowd grows. Wander off and the music winds down on its own.

### 🎧 The DJ knows when you're there — your way

This is the clever part, and **you choose how it works** — all of it runs **on your own machine, nothing ever leaves it:**

- **⌨️ Activity** _(default)_ — the DJ wakes when you use your keyboard or mouse. No permission, no sensors, totally private. Perfect if you just want it running while you work.
- **🎙️ Microphone** — wakes to the sound of the room (a loudness reading only — no recording, no listening-in). Best with headphones.
- **📷 Camera** — wakes when a webcam sees a face. 100% on-device face detection; **no camera frames ever leave your computer.**
- **🚫 Off** — just plays. No sensing at all.

### ✨ What's inside

- **40 hand-drawn venues** — a neon club, a rooftop at golden hour, a moonlit beach, a hot-air balloon, a desert rave… each a bespoke little scene.
- **Real day/night** — outdoor spots follow your actual clock (and if you overstay the park after dark… the cops might show up 🚓).
- **A soundtrack that never runs dry** — 89 Creative-Commons tracks to start, plus one-tap **music packs** and a built-in search that pulls more free CC music from the Internet Archive. You'll never hit "I've heard this."
- **Bring your own** — drop in your files or paste any internet-radio stream.
- **Cozy progression** — earn as you build a healthy practice, unlock venues, grow your crowd. Calm mode hides all of it if you just want the music.
- **Pick your size** — Small / Medium / Large HUD, so it reads on any monitor.

### 🚫 No AI slop

The art is **hand-coded** pixel by pixel. The music is **made by real humans** (all Creative-Commons, every artist credited in-app). The *only* machine-learning here is the optional on-device presence sensor — and that stays on your device. No generated art, no generated music, no data leaving your machine.

## Camera / microphone privacy note (put this in the description AND itch's "more info")

> **Your privacy, plainly:** Pixel DJ's presence feature can use your keyboard/mouse, your microphone, or your camera — **you pick, and it defaults to keyboard/mouse.** If you choose the camera or microphone, all processing happens **locally in your browser**. No video, no audio, and no images are ever recorded, saved, uploaded, or sent anywhere. There is no account, no tracking, and no analytics. The camera/mic options exist only so the little DJ knows when you've stepped away.
