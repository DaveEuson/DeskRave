# 🎧 Musicians — get your tracks into Desk Rave

Desk Rave is a tiny 16-bit pixel-art club that lives on people's desks and
**reacts to music in real time** — the bass drives the crowd, the highs shimmer
the lights, a pixel DJ works the decks. People leave it running all day while
they work. Your track doesn't just get *played* — it gets a **room that dances
to it**.

The soundtrack is 100% human-made, Creative-Commons music, and **every artist is
credited**. If that's you, we'd love your tracks.

## What you get

- **Credit everywhere your track appears:** artist + title in the now-playing
  display while it plays, a per-artist credit (with a link to your site/page) in
  the app's Options, and a per-track entry in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).
- Listeners who literally stare at a scene dancing to your music for hours.
- You **keep all rights** to your music. CC licensing is non-exclusive — you can
  keep selling/streaming it anywhere.

**Honest up front:** Desk Rave is a free project. There's no payment — this is
credit + reach, nothing else. If that's not worth it for you, that's completely
fair.

## What we're looking for

The venues want **instrumental electronic** music in these lanes:

`house` · `techno` · `trance` · `drum & bass` · `synthwave` · `lofi` · `chill` ·
`downtempo` · `ambient`

- Instrumental (or minimal vocals) — it plays while people focus.
- Roughly 2–6 minutes a track (longer mixes are welcome too — they work like a
  radio station in-app).
- Finished, mastered-enough tracks. It doesn't need to be fancy — it needs to
  groove.

## The requirements (the license is the whole deal)

1. **It must be your own original work.** No samples of copyrighted material, no
   covers or remixes of commercial songs (even if your arrangement is original,
   the underlying composition isn't yours to license).
2. **You license it as [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
   or [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).** Plain CC-BY
   or CC0 only — we can't take NC / SA / ND variants, because the app
   redistributes the audio.
3. **No AI-generated music.** Desk Rave's whole pitch is "no AI slop — real
   humans, credited." AI-assisted *tools* in your workflow are your business;
   tracks that are substantially AI-generated don't fit here.

## How to submit

**Open a [music submission issue](../../issues/new?template=music-submission.md)**
on this repo with:

- Artist name + a link for your credit (site, Bandcamp, archive.org, wherever)
- A listen link (archive.org, Bandcamp, SoundCloud, direct file…)
- The genre lane(s) you'd put it in
- A line confirming: *"This is my own original work and I license it under
  CC BY 4.0 / CC0."*

The easiest hosting for us is an **archive.org upload** (the app can stream
straight from it), but anywhere we can download from works — bundled tracks
ship inside the app itself.

We listen to everything. If it fits a venue, it goes in with full credit.
